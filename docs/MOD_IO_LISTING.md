# Advanced Bank Search

## Summary
Search your bank with RuneQL, build and save queries, organize them into folders, and share query codes.

## Description
Advanced Bank Search adds a Search sidebar to your bank. Find items by name, stack quantity, GE price, and RuneQL catalog fields. Create queries with the visual builder or type RuneQL directly, save your searches, choose item icons, and organize them into folders. Import and export individual queries or a complete query backup.

Requires PrettyUI, Bank Core, RuneQL, and GE Prices. Enable all dependencies in the plugin manager, open your bank, and click Search on the left rail.

### Data protection
Advanced Bank Search stores saved queries, folders, icons, and preferences locally through the client's persistent storage. It does not send your bank contents or saved queries to an external service. Query codes contain the searches and metadata you choose to share; share them only with people you intend to receive that information. Required dependency plugins manage their own data and network activity.
